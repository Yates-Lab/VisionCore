    \textbf{Fixational eye movements engage V1 spatiotemporal passbands and
    sharpen spatial coding.}
    \textbf{(A)} A natural-image history translated by a filtered measured
    fixation trajectory and its lag-aligned stabilized counterpart were passed
    through the same model. The example unit increases both firing rate and
    single-spike information (SSI); the difference map is measured motion minus
    stabilized response.
    \textbf{(B)} Population firing-rate and SSI modulation versus filtered
    250-ms fixation path length for all 725 checkpoint-available exact
    readouts. Boxes show the distribution of unit effects, whiskers span the
    5th--95th percentiles, and curves show population estimates with bootstrap
    intervals.
    \textbf{(C)} Equal-dynamic-mass natural-image power distributions for
    drift-rich and rapid-transient filtered fixation ensembles. Rapid dynamics
    shift relative power toward higher temporal frequencies.
    \textbf{(D)} Raw blank-subtracted responses of two strictly validated model
    units to controlled drifting gratings. Stars mark fitted SF$\times$TF
    preferences and curves show half-maximum Yu-fit passbands.
    \textbf{(E)} Density of the fitted passbands for all 725
    checkpoint-available exact readouts. The all-unit display retains units that
    fail the stricter exemplar-validation gates.
    \textbf{(F)} Rapid-transient minus drift-rich log$_2$ power contrast with
    the same example passbands as in (D).
    \textbf{(G)} Direct measured-motion minus stabilized firing-rate and SSI
    modulation versus within-unit passband-power percentile. Boxes show
    unit-specific bin medians; curves and intervals summarize the population.
    \textbf{(H)} Gain-invariant temporal modulation and spatial sharpening for
    top-passband movies traced through cumulative trained readout branches.
    The final point is the ordinary model output; no affine, ablated, or
    synthetic reference model is used. Production inference requires at least
    100 crossed image--fixation movies.
