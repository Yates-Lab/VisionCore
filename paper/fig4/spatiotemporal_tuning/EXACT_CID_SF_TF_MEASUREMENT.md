# Figure 4 SF/TF measurement contract

## Why the previous measurement was not releasable

Figure 4 needs two distinct facts and must not collapse them into one label.

1. The recorded forage-grating experiment measures biological spatial
   frequency, orientation, retinal phase, and a response-lag profile. The
   processed `gratings.dset` has no independently varied temporal-frequency
   field, and the configured sessions contain no usable drifting-grating
   condition. The response-lag profile is not temporal-frequency tuning.
2. Temporal-frequency tuning can therefore be assayed only in the model,
   using controlled synthetic drifting gratings. It is a model
   inference, not a biological TF measurement.

Earlier Figure 4 drafts additionally mixed inherited RR100 representatives,
half-circle motion sampling, different response metrics, pseudo-baselines, and
unconstrained peak or contour estimates. Those choices can produce duplicated
identities, boundary peaks, bimodal surfaces, and visually persuasive lassos
that do not follow the measured grid.

## Current measurement

`run_exact_cid_drifting_tuning.py` replays gratings through the checkpoint's
native per-session readout for each exact `(session, cid)` identity. It never
constructs or scores an RR100 population head.

- Model: checkpoint and label declared by
  `paper/model_selection/production_model.yaml`.
- Input: 35 pixels at 37.504766 pixels/degree and 240 Hz.
- History: all 60 native lags (250 ms), with lag zero as the current frame.
- SF: nine cycle-valid samples from 1.0716 to 15.9395 cycles/degree. The lowest
  sample contains one full cycle across the aperture; the highest remains below
  85% of spatial Nyquist.
- TF: a gray/static condition plus fourteen half-octave samples from 1 to
  90.5097 Hz, below 80% of temporal Nyquist.
- Motion: 18 translation directions spanning the full 360 degrees.
- Phase: 24 uniformly spaced endpoint phases.
- Behavior: held at zero.
- Primary response: phase-averaged expected count (F0) minus the explicit gray
  blank. Values are expected spikes per 1/240-second output bin; multiplying by
  240 gives spikes/second.
- Diagnostics: F1, F2, phase RMS, all-harmonic RMS, extrema, and independent
  12-phase estimates are saved but are not substituted for F0.

Uniform endpoint-phase sampling is the steady periodic assay for this
finite-history feed-forward model: every possible phase of a 60-frame drifting
history is presented directly, without adding recurrent state or an arbitrary
discard period.

## Release audit

`audit_exact_cid_drifting_tuning.py` fails closed. A unit is eligible only if:

- its native `(session, cid)` mapping is unique and exact;
- the complete physical SF x TF x direction grid is present;
- 24- and 12-phase estimates agree in surface, peak SF, peak TF, and direction;
- blank-subtracted F0 drive is positive;
- the raw peak is inside the sampled SF and TF support;
- the raw surface is coherent rather than fragmented;
- a Yu R0/R1 fit over every acquired dynamic cell has R-squared at least 0.80,
  an interior peak, and a peak within 0.75 octave of the raw maximum;
- its raw surface and peak repeat at an independently replayed 12.5% contrast;
- the matched biological unit has reliable, non-boundary, split-half recorded
  SF tuning; and
- the model reproduces that recorded SF curve sufficiently well.

The Yu fit supplies the display lasso and the continuous passband weight used
for spectral engagement. Every exemplar must show the raw low-resolution
response grid beside the lasso. A fit cannot make an excluded unit usable.

The release report, every-unit atlas, candidate diagnostics, and exact failure
reasons are written to the explicitly requested output directory.

## Downstream Figure 4 rules

- Panel B uses all checkpoint-available exact readouts and does not split by
  SF; its path-length result is independent of tuning release.
- Population TF and SF x TF passbands must be labeled as model-inferred
  tuning, not measured biological TF.
- Panel D exemplars require `validated_for_figure4=True`. Panels E--H may use
  all checkpoint-available exact readouts only through the explicitly labeled
  all-unit contract, which retains and reports the strict-validation failures.
- Contrasting examples require the recorded neuron and exact model readout to occupy the
  same outer SF third, while the model occupies the opposite TF extreme. After
  all quality gates, the low-SF/high-TF example is the crossed-extremity leader
  and the high-SF/low-TF example is the medoid of its audited cluster in log
  SFxTF space. Both choices are deterministic, tuning-only, and independent of
  retinal-motion responses.
- Figure 4 is not regenerated by the measurement scripts. The figure builder
  must explicitly consume the release table and fail if the report says
  `figure4_unblocked=false`.
